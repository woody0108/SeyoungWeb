ABOUT SEYOUNG

Built for demanding plant projects

Seyoung Industry is a specialized fabricator of fired heaters and plant equipment, providing welding, quality control, delivery and field support.

Our refinery and petrochemical experience enables coordinated execution from material review and fabrication through inspection, shipment and site assistance. We work to project specifications, documentation requirements and delivery priorities.

PRODUCTS & SERVICES

From fabrication to field support

We fabricate critical plant equipment and components to project specifications and quality requirements, with practical support through delivery and site execution.

01

Engineering Support

From drawings and specifications to build-ready fabrication planning, we support each project with practical technical review and procedure preparation.

02

Manufacturing

Built under ISO and ASME-based quality systems, our fabrication capability covers critical equipment, components and plant steelwork.

03

Welding

Pressure-part, piping and structural welding are carried out by qualified personnel according to project specifications and approved procedures.

04

Inspection & Testing

Weld inspection, dimensional checks and hydrostatic testing help verify safety, quality and compliance before delivery.

05

Field Service & Maintenance

On-site installation, shutdown maintenance, modification and replacement support are available for operating plant requirements.

06

Packing & Delivery

Protected packing, delivery preparation and transport coordination keep fabricated items ready for reliable shipment to project sites.
